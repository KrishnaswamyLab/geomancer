# Code of conduct

Treat contributors and users with respect. Welcome questions, give specific and
constructive feedback, and credit others' work. Harassment, discrimination,
threats, and disclosure of private information are unacceptable.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and restrict participation to keep
the project welcoming. Report concerns privately to a repository maintainer using
their published contact details; do not disclose sensitive reports in public issues.
Maintainers will handle reports discreetly and recuse themselves from conflicts.
